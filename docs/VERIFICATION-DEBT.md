# Verification debt — what has been proven, and what still needs a physical sample

*Opened 2026-09-03. Restructured 2026-09-04 by lane V1. The rule this file
exists to enforce: **a tool is not working because a document says so; it is
working because it ran and produced a number.** MISSION.md requires every
artifact in the release pack to be generated by a tool on this machine and
graded PASS. This page tracks the gap between what the repository claims and
what has actually executed.*

**Every row below was re-run by lane V1 on 2026-09-04. Nothing here is closed
on another lane's report.** Where a number appears, it is the number that came
back on this machine, with the command beside it.

## Why this page exists

On 2026-09-03 the autorouter was found in the toolchain documentation as a
working step of the board pipeline. The jar was downloaded, the wrapper script
was written, and the workflow document described the command. **It had never
once run**, because this Mac has no Java runtime — `/usr/bin/java` is Apple's
stub, which prints "Unable to locate a Java Runtime" and exits. Nothing in the
project noticed, because nothing had asked it to route a board and checked the
result.

That is the failure mode this page is for: not a bug, but a claim that was never
tested. It is exactly what would sink a factory release pack, because the pack
is a set of claims about a board nobody has built.

## V7 — the sounder reaches 60 Phon at 25 cm
**Maps to `TEST-PLAN.md` QUAL-04. Unlocks production test FU-03, which is
CANNOT DETERMINE until this is done.**

**Why it cannot be simulated.** Phon is a loudness *level* computed from a
measured spectrum under ISO 532-1:2017. It is not a sound-pressure level and it
is not dB(A). No circuit simulation produces it, because it depends on the
bonded bender, the shell's cavity, the port geometry and the room. `ce-spice`
models the *drive current* into the bender and nothing beyond the terminals.

**Load-bearing.** DULT §3.13.3 makes 60 Phon mandatory for an accessory that can
be separated from its owner. `docs/ANTI-STALKING.md` §4.1 and `research/06`
constraint C8 carry the requirement. The only published AirTag figure lane G
could find is **78–80 dB at about 13 cm**, which is a different quantity at a
different distance and cannot be converted.

**Equipment**
- Class 1 sound level meter or measurement microphone with a **calibrated free-field response**, e.g. a ½″ prepolarised condenser capsule with a Class 1 preamplifier.
- An **ISO 532-1:2017 loudness analyser** — Zwicker method, time-varying. A meter that reports only dB(A) cannot close this row. Software implementations are acceptable if the implementation names ISO 532-1:2017 and its version.
- Acoustic calibrator, 94 dB at 1 kHz, with a calibration certificate inside its stated interval.
- A room or chamber whose background is **at least 10 dB below the lowest one-third-octave band of the measurement**, measured, not assumed.
- Fixture `FX-5` from `TEST-PLAN.md` §4 for the production correlation step.

**Setup**
1. Calibrate the microphone with the acoustic calibrator immediately before and immediately after the session. **If the two readings differ by more than 0.3 dB, the session is void.**
2. Mount the sealed unit with its sounder port facing the microphone, on axis, with the acoustic centre of the capsule **250 ± 5 mm** from the outer face of the shell. Support the unit on a low-reflection mount, not on a bench top.
3. Record the background spectrum with the unit present but silent. Record it in one-third-octave bands.
4. Command the sounder over GATT exactly as `TEST-PLAN.md` FU-04 does, with the unit on its own cell and no wires. **The cell must be a fresh cell of the shipped brand and lot** — a bench supply changes the result, because the sounder's output tracks the rail under the piezo's reactive load.
5. Capture at least **10 s** of the sounding pattern, including its silent gaps. ISO 532-1 time-varying loudness returns a percentile; take **N5** (the level exceeded 5 % of the time), and state that you did.

**Sample size.** **At least 5 golden units**, per QUAL-04, so the golden reading
is a mean and not one unit's luck. Report the mean and the sample standard
deviation. If the standard deviation exceeds 3 phon, the bond process is not
under control and the mean is not yet a golden number.

**Pass**
- **N5 loudness ≥ 60 phon**, computed under ISO 532-1:2017, at 250 mm on axis, on the mean of ≥ 5 units, **and** the weakest single unit ≥ 57 phon.
- Background ≥ 10 dB below the measurement in every contributing band, stated as a number.
- Both calibrator readings within 0.3 dB of each other.

**What it also produces, and must not be skipped.** In the same session, record
the **A-weighted SPL at 25 cm** for each unit. That number, minus a tolerance
from a gauge R&R study on `FX-5`'s hood and meter, becomes FU-03's production
limit. **Nobody may write 60 into a dB(A) cell.** `TEST-PLAN.md` line 469 says
so and this page agrees: they are different quantities and swapping them is
exactly the error `docs/TOOLS-THAT-LIE.md` exists to stop.

## V5 — the firmware advertises correctly, on a real radio
**Related to `TEST-PLAN.md` AB-11 (production) and QUAL-05 (certification), but
this debt is neither: it is the first proof that our silicon transmits at all.**

**Where it stands.** Proven **in emulation only**. `ce-fwsim` verifies the Find
My advertisement field by field against SPEC F1 from the emulated firmware's
UART log. Lane V1's audit found the specific limit of that proof: the checks
read a printed log line, and **nothing reads the radio's own `ready=` / `end=` /
`disabled=` fields**, so a firmware whose `radio_tx()` returns without the RADIO
peripheral raising a single event still passes. No electromagnetic wave has
existed.

**Equipment**
- 1 assembled halo board on its own CR2032, in its carrier (station-2 state), plus 1 sealed unit.
- A BLE 5 sniffer that reports the **raw PDU**, not a decoded name: nRF Sniffer for Bluetooth LE on an nRF52840 dongle with Wireshark, or a Ellisys/Frontline analyser. A phone app is not acceptable — it cannot show you the bytes.
- An RF-quiet enclosure (`FX-3`) with a **leakage self-test**: with a powered halo 300 mm *outside* the closed box, the in-box scanner must see **zero** of its advertisements in a 20 s capture. Run it at the start of every session. A box that fails invalidates every capture since the last pass.
- An iPhone or iPad on a current iOS with Find My enabled, and a second one signed into a different Apple ID, for the location-report half.
- The unit's provisioned private key, on the programming station only. **It must not leave that station**; record the SHA-256 fingerprint of the public key instead (`TEST-PLAN.md` line 190).

**Setup — part A, the advertisement exists on air**
1. Run the box leakage self-test. Record the result with the timestamp.
2. Power the unit on its own cell. Capture **20 s** of advertising channels 37, 38 and 39.
3. Export the raw PDUs. For each captured advertisement, check the 31 bytes against `ce-fwsim/tools/findmy_decode.py`'s field list: `len=0x1e`, `type=0xff`, company `4c 00`, Apple type `0x12`, OF length `0x19`, key bytes at `[7:29]`, key high bits at `[29]`, hint `[30]=0x00`.
4. **Derive the advertised key independently**: recover it from the captured bytes plus the captured advertising address, and compare its SHA-256 to the fingerprint recorded at programming. This is the step emulation cannot do, because in emulation the key on the wire and the key in the log come from the same process.
5. Leave the unit running and capture again after **> 15 minutes** to observe a key rotation on air.

**Setup — part B, a location report comes back**
6. Take the unit **out of the box**, at least 200 m from the owner phone, in a place with other iPhones passing.
7. Wait for the separated state and its DULT advertising, then query Apple's Find My network for reports against the day's derived hashed public keys.
8. A report is closed when a decrypted location, its timestamp and its accuracy come back and the timestamp is **after** the unit was placed.

**Sample size.** **3 units** for part A. **1 unit** for part B, repeated on **3
separate days**, because the network's reporting density is not under our
control and one silent day is not evidence of a broken tag.

**Pass**
- Part A: **≥ 5 advertisements decoded per channel in 20 s**, all 31 bytes matching SPEC F1, and the recovered key's SHA-256 equal to the programming fingerprint — on 3 of 3 units.
- Part A rotation: the key on air at t+15 min **differs** from the key at t=0, and both are members of the sequence `ce-fwsim/tools/rollkey_ref.py` derives from the master beacon key read out of the ELF image.
- Part B: **at least one decrypted location report** whose timestamp falls inside the separation window, on **2 of 3 days**. One report is the whole claim; the second day exists to distinguish a working tag from a lucky one.
- **FAIL, explicitly, if any advertisement is seen but no report ever comes back** — that is a payload that is well-formed and useless, and it is the outcome this debt exists to catch.

## V8 — Channel Sounding reaches 6–20 cm between two halos
**Maps to `TEST-PLAN.md` QUAL-10. SPEC §2 F10.**

**Where it stands.** The 6–10 cm figure is from a **published study on other
hardware** (nRF54L15 development kits), not on ours. `DECISIONS.md` D12 carries
the known gap in the open: Channel Sounding degrades from 25 cm to **331 cm**
when channels collide without deterministic scheduling, and a single-antenna
device can lose metres to orientation.

**Equipment**
- **8 halo units**, per QUAL-10 — the same configuration as the published study, so ours is comparable to theirs.
- A room with at least **10 m** of clear line of sight and no metal within 1 m of the path.
- A **measured** ground-truth baseline: a laser distance meter with a stated accuracy of ≤ 1 mm, or a surveyed tape. Chalk marks on a floor are not a baseline.
- Non-metallic stands that place each unit at **1.0 m** height, and a rotation stage or a marked protractor for the orientation sweep.

**Setup**
1. Place unit A at the origin. Place unit B at each of **9 true distances**: 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8.5 and 10 m, each measured with the laser and recorded to the millimetre.
2. At each distance, take **30 ranging estimates** at the shipped 30 s update rate, over ≥ 15 minutes.
3. Repeat the whole sweep at **4 relative orientations** of unit B: 0°, 90°, 180°, 270° about its vertical axis. Orientation is the failure mode D12 names; a sweep at one orientation measures the best case and calls it the answer.
4. Repeat the whole sweep **once with a second pair of units in the room ranging simultaneously**, to reproduce the channel-collision case. Record whether the firmware schedules deterministically.
5. Record every estimate, not a mean — the distribution is the result.

**Sample size.** 8 units → **4 independent pairs**. 9 distances × 4 orientations
× 30 estimates × 4 pairs = **4 320 estimates**. Anything less does not separate a
median from a tail.

**Pass**
- **Mean absolute error ≤ 20 cm** across all distances ≤ 10 m, at every orientation, with no single pair worse than 30 cm.
- **95th-percentile absolute error ≤ 50 cm.** The mean alone hides the failure D12 warns about.
- The collision run is reported **as its own number** and is not averaged into the clean one. If it exceeds 1 m, that is a finding about scheduling and it goes to DECISIONS.md — **do not widen this row's limit to absorb it.**
- FAIL if fewer than 90 % of scheduled ranging attempts return an estimate at all: a tag that ranges accurately when it answers and answers half the time has not passed.

## V4 — the coin-cell model reflects a real cell
**Related to `TEST-PLAN.md` QUAL-03 (battery life) and production test AB-07
(sleep current), but distinct from both: this closes the *model*, not the life.**

**Where it stands.** `ce-spice/examples/cr2032_pulse_load` passes against its own
datasheet-derived assertions. Lane V1's audit found what those assertions do not
cover: **12 of them hold on a flat waveform.** `v_min_V > 1.8`, `droop_mV < 400`
and `i_peak_mA < 8.0` are one floor and two ceilings, and a cell that is never
loaded satisfies all three. Nothing asserts the 8 mA pulse happened.

**Equipment**
- A **source-measure unit** or a precision ammeter able to resolve **1 µA to 20 mA in one sweep without range-changing mid-capture** — a range change during the transmit pulse is indistinguishable from the pulse.
- An oscilloscope with a **differential probe across the cell terminals**, ≥ 100 kHz bandwidth, DC-coupled.
- A four-wire (Kelvin) cell holder. `TEST-PLAN.md` §6 records that `TP_VDD_FORCE`/`TP_VDD_SENSE` and `TP_GND_FORCE`/`TP_GND_SENSE` are **still a request to lane B1**; without them this measurement cannot be made on the board and must be made in a cell fixture instead.
- A climate chamber for the temperature corners, or at minimum a stable 23 ± 2 °C room with the temperature logged.
- Cells: **fresh**, **half-discharged** and **end-of-life** samples of the shipped brand and lot. End-of-life is defined by charge withdrawn, not by voltage: discharge to **90 % of the datasheet's rated 220 mAh** at a low constant current and record the coulombs.

**Setup**
1. Measure each cell's **open-circuit voltage** and its **AC impedance at 1 kHz** before the pulse test. The impedance is the number the SPICE model's ESR must match; the datasheet gives a typical, and a typical is not a measurement.
2. Load the cell with the board's real transmit pattern — not a synthetic square wave. Trigger the scope on the current burst.
3. Capture **V(cell) and I(cell) simultaneously** through at least 20 consecutive advertising events.
4. Repeat at **−20 °C, +23 °C and +60 °C**, the product range in SPEC §4. Note the conflict QUAL-07 records: the Maxell datasheet gives −20 to +85 °C for the cell and SPEC §4 gives −20 to +60 °C for the product.
5. Re-run `ce-spice/examples/cr2032_pulse_load` with the **measured** ESR and OCV substituted for the datasheet ones, and compare the simulated droop to the measured droop.

**Sample size.** **5 cells per state of charge** (fresh / half / end-of-life)
**per temperature corner** = 45 cells. Cell-to-cell ESR spread is the dominant
term and one cell is a rumour.

**Pass**
- **Measured minimum V(cell) during the pulse ≥ 1.8 V** at every corner, including end-of-life at −20 °C, which is the worst case and the only one that matters.
- **Simulated droop within 20 % of measured droop** at every corner. That is the assertion that closes this row: the debt is about the *model*, and a model that agrees with the datasheet but not with the cell is the thing being tested.
- **Measured peak current within 15 % of the 8 mA the model assumes.** If it is not, the model's premise is wrong and the droop agreement above is a coincidence.
- Record the measured ESR beside the datasheet ESR. If they differ by more than 30 %, raise it as a finding — **do not re-fit the model's ESR to make the droop agree**, because that closes the row by deleting the test.

## V14 — *(new)* the antenna resonates, on a real board in a real chamber
**Opened by lane V1, 2026-09-04. Related to QUAL-05 but not the same test.**

**Why it is here.** V1 measured the simulated antenna and found no resonance at
all — see V12 below. The simulation debt is closeable on this machine; the
question *does the physical antenna resonate in the BLE band* is not.

**Equipment**
- A **2-port vector network analyser** covering 1–6 GHz, calibrated with a
  traceable SOLT or ECal standard **on the day**, with the calibration verified
  by measuring a known 50 Ω load and a short.
- A **semi-rigid coaxial pigtail** soldered to the antenna feed with the shield
  bonded to the ground pour within 2 mm of the feed. A flexible cable moves the
  measurement; a long ground path adds inductance and shifts the answer.
- An anechoic or reverberation chamber with a calibrated reference antenna for
  the gain and efficiency half.
- The unit in **three states**: bare board, board in carrier, and sealed unit
  with its cell fitted. The cell's copper is inside the antenna's near field.

**Setup**
1. Sweep **1–6 GHz**, ≥ 1001 points, and export the full S11 **as complex data**, not as a magnitude plot.
2. **Find the reactance zero-crossing.** Report the frequency at which `Im(Z)` crosses zero going upward. That is the resonance. A minimum in |S11| is not a resonance and must not be reported as one — this is the exact error V12 records in the simulation.
3. Report |S11| at 2.402, 2.440 and 2.480 GHz separately, not just the minimum.
4. Measure realized gain and total radiated power in the chamber at 2.440 GHz against the reference antenna.
5. Repeat all of the above in each of the three assembly states.

**Sample size.** **5 units**, and both a bare board and a sealed unit from each,
because the shell and the cell are inside the near field and the debt is about
the shipped configuration.

**Pass**
- A **reactance zero-crossing inside 2.400–2.4835 GHz** on 5 of 5 sealed units. **No zero-crossing is a FAIL, whatever |S11| says.**
- **|S11| ≤ −6 dB across the whole band** 2.400–2.4835 GHz, worst point, sealed.
- **Realized gain ≥ −6 dBi** at 2.440 GHz, sealed. Apple's published figure is −3.2 dBi and that remains the target in `spec/convergence.json`; −6 dBi is the floor at which the link budget still closes. **Do not move the −3.2 dBi target to meet a measurement.**
- Report the **implied effective permittivity** from the measured resonance and the physical arm length. **It must exceed 1.0.** If it does not, the measurement is wrong, because a dielectric cannot speed a wave up.

## The rule for every lane

When a lane reports something works, the report says **which command it ran and
what number came back**. "The pipeline is wired up" is not a result. A lane that
cannot run its step says CANNOT DETERMINE and names what is missing — which is
how V1 was found, and how V2, V3, V12, V13 and V15–V18 were found after it.

**Never close a row by weakening its check.** A row closed by loosening a limit
is worse than an open row, because it stops anyone looking. `ce-rf`'s
`eps_eff_implied` floor going from 1.0 to 0.64 is the example to learn from: it
turned a physical law into a tolerance, and an impossible antenna went green.

**Prove every new check by breaking something on purpose.**
`python3 tools/prove_checks.py` does exactly that for the three checkers on this
page — 25 of 25 assertions seen to fire — and any check added here is expected
to earn a case in it.

## The mirror defect: a capability declared ABSENT because the probe looked in the wrong place

*Added 2026-09-05, after it was recorded as fact twice.*

Everything else on this page and in `TOOLS-THAT-LIE.md` is a check that
**passed** while measuring the wrong thing. This is the same defect inverted: a
check that **failed** while measuring the wrong thing, and it cost more than any
false pass has.

**Twice** a lane reported that this machine has no Java runtime and therefore no
autorouter, and twice it was relayed onward as a finding. Both times the
evidence was `java -version` returning *"Unable to locate a Java Runtime"*, and
once also a search for the jar under `~/dev/ce-workshop` that found nothing.

Both observations are true and neither answers the question. Apple ships a
**stub** at `/usr/bin/java` that prints exactly that message whether or not a
runtime is installed, and Homebrew's `openjdk` is **keg-only**, so it is
deliberately not on `PATH`. The runtime is at
`/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk/bin/java` and answers `openjdk version "26.0.2.1"`.
The jar is at `~/.local/share/freerouting/freerouting-2.3.0.jar`, outside the
workshop tree.

**The measurement that settles it in one command:** `ce-pcb/bin/route --doctor`
exits 0 and prints a row reading **`jar runs → Freerouting v2.3.0`** — a row that
exists precisely because a path is not a running program.

**The rule.** Before reporting a capability absent, ask **the tool that owns the
question**, not the environment. A `--doctor` verb exists for this, and where one
does not exist, write it. "I could not find it" is a statement about the search,
not about the machine — and a false negative burns a night exactly as surely as
a false positive burns a factory run.

## The 2.4 GHz element has never been solved with the coil that sits under it

*Opened 2026-09-05 by lane B1. Anchor: `out/verify/routed-check.json` R9.*

`ce-rf/out/halo-rev-a-2g4/model.json` — the case that models **this board's own
element** — carries `"passive_copper": []`. It solves the arm with **no NFC coil
present at all**. It returns 2.4927 GHz at a radiation resistance of **2.71 Ω**,
which lane T3 flagged as suspicious.

Measured on halo_rev_a's copper, in plan view across all layers, with the
matching network exempted:

| net | closest approach to the arm |
|---|--:|
| **NFC1** | **−0.1746 mm** — the coil's copper **overlaps** the arm |
| **NFC2** | **+0.1473 mm** — inside the 0.30 mm floor |
| GND | +1.4928 mm |
| VDD | +10.3810 mm |

Lane T3's own numbers say what that is worth: the Ø25.2 mm coil at a 0.30 mm
gap solves to **2.4321 GHz**, in band; the same coil **under the arm** solves to
**1.7666 GHz**, a **668 MHz** shift they state is not recoverable by tuning.

**So the number on the convergence table for this board's antenna is a
measurement of a board we are not building.** Not because the solver is wrong —
because the model is missing the largest piece of passive copper within a
millimetre of the radiator.

**And the coil is not the only passive copper the model is missing.** The new
`antenna-arm-shadow` keep-out found a second one on its first run: **BT1.2, the
cell's negative contact land**, GND, on B.Cu at (23.046, 7.200) — directly under
the arm. That land is where a CR2032's rim actually touches, so it is
mechanical and neither this lane's nor the router's to move. It is excluded
from the keep-out for that reason and recorded here instead, because it belongs
to the same question: **`passive_copper: []` is missing at least two large
grounded conductors within a millimetre of the radiator.**

**What would settle it**, in order:

1. Re-solve `halo-rev-a-2g4` with the real coil as `passive_copper`, on the
   Ø26.00 × 0.60 mm outline. Until then this board's resonance is CANNOT
   DETERMINE, and 2.4927 GHz must not be quoted for it.
2. If it lands where the overlap predicts, the fix is layout, not tuning: the
   arm and the coil have to stop sharing the same 3 mm of annulus in plan view.
   That is lane B1's copper and lane C's floorplan together.
3. **Do not transfer T3's "shorten the open-end tail by 1.5 mm" to this board
   without re-solving.** It was solved on the **Ø30 × 1.00 mm study puck**,
   where the tail is 2.5 mm and the trim removes 60 % of it and 4.7 % of the
   conductor. **This board's tail is 1.4539 mm**, so 1.5 mm is 103 % of the
   tail and 6.1 % of the conductor — it would delete the tail entirely and cut
   0.046 mm into the last meander tooth. Different outline, different
   thickness, different eps_eff, and `halo-rim-ifa-coil-retuned`'s own
   `verdict.json` currently reads **CANNOT DETERMINE**. The recommendation may
   well be right in direction; the number does not transfer, and this project
   does not carry numbers across geometries without re-solving.
