halo — open AirTag-compatible tracker

Factory handoff pack · generated 2026-09-03 · commit 39a9725

halo is an open-source tracker that behaves like an Apple AirTag: it is found through Apple's Find My network, it makes a sound, a phone can tap it, and it wakes on motion. Every design file is public. This pack states exactly what is finished, what is not, and what we need a factory to measure that a computer cannot.

halo 是一个开源追踪器，功能对标 Apple AirTag：可通过 Apple Find My 网络定位、可发声、支持手机 NFC 触碰读取、运动唤醒。全部设计文件公开。本文件说明：哪些已完成、哪些未完成，以及哪些必须由工厂实测、计算机无法给出答案。

3 · What we need from you

These cannot be settled on a computer. They are the reason a real factory changes this project more than any amount of further research would.

QuestionWhy it matters
Can you measure sound pressure against a calibrated meter?
能否用校准声级计测量声压？防跟踪标准要求 25 cm 处 60 Phon。		The anti-stalking standard mandates 60 Phon at 25 cm. We chose a bare piezo bender bonded to the shell because every housed buzzer is 3.5 mm tall and we have 1.5 mm. Whether that reaches the required loudness is a measurement, not a simulation, and it is the single largest open risk in the product.
Does the Ø31.87 by 7.98 mm stack close in moulded plastic?
Ø31.87 × 7.98 mm 的堆叠在注塑件中是否可行？		A coin cell plus the internal module already consumes 6.5 mm of 7.98 mm. About 1.5 mm remains for the cover, clearances and shell wall.
What opening force does your bayonet tooling achieve?
卡扣式电池门的开启力是多少？		United States law requires a coin-cell compartment to need a tool or two independent simultaneous movements. A snap fit fails outright.
What is your process capability on a Ø32 mm four-layer board?
Ø32 mm 四层板的工艺能力参数？		We need minimum trace and space, via drill and annular ring, edge clearance, and whether castellated edges are possible at this size, so we can encode them as automated checks.
Can you source the Nordic nRF54L10 at volume, and does a pre-certified module exist on it?
能否批量采购 nRF54L10？是否有基于该芯片的预认证模组？		This chip gives us centimetre-level ranging between our own devices for less money than the older part. If it cannot be sourced, that decision reverses.
Do you have a network analyser for antenna measurement?
是否有矢量网络分析仪用于天线实测？		Our solver is validated to within 0.66 percent of a published reference, but a simulated antenna is not a measured one.

任何位置不得使用 Apple 商标。		Not on the product, the packaging, the documentation or the listing. Apple's guidelines bar even a phonetic equivalent or abbreviation. 防跟踪功能属于必备物料，不可选配。		The sound maker, the accelerometer and the connectable advertisement are required by the industry standard that Apple and Google jointly authored. 5 · Open risks, stated plainly

  • The antenna does not yet resonate in the Bluetooth band — Two round-board geometries return a passing verdict while resonating at 4.0 and 5.8 GHz, because their assertions test only how deep the match is and how efficient the radiator is, never the frequency. Caught by the orchestrator on 2026-09-03, the assertion is being made mandatory and the geometry retuned. This is stated here rather than hidden because a passing test that checks the wrong thing is the most dangerous artifact in any release pack.
  • The sounder is unproven and legally required — See question one. If a bonded bender cannot reach 60 Phon at 25 cm, the fallback is a micro-speaker, which costs more than the rest of the board.
  • Nothing has been built — Every result in this pack comes from a simulator or an emulator. Eight claims are tracked in docs/VERIFICATION-DEBT.md with the measurement that would close each; four of them need hardware.
