What halo is

An open copy of the Apple AirTag: a Ø31.874 × 7.980 mm puck that a factory could build. Inside it is a Ø26.00 mm four-layer 0.60 mm board carrying a Nordic nRF54L10, an NFC coil wound in bottom copper, a piezo sounder, a CR2032 on three sprung contacts, and a 2.4 GHz antenna etched into the board's own top copper. It advertises on Apple's Find My network. It does not do precision finding — that needs Apple's U1, which nobody else can buy — and it is not a visual replica of the AirTag; it is a function-for-function one, on a board redrawn rather than copied so no licence follows it.

Nothing has been moulded, stamped or printed. Every picture on this site is a render of a solid or a plot of a solve, and every one of them says how old it is.

What is open

The things that would stop this shipping, in the order they matter. None of them is hidden anywhere else on this site.

  • The antenna does not meet its own spec, and does not beat Apple. latest solve halo-rev-a-2g4-rt1-passive: -11.806 dBi at 2.3730 GHz. Best ever measured on this element: -3.391 dBi at 2.0669 GHz (halo-rev-a-2g4-meander9-passive). Apple's filed figure is −3.2 dBi, the band is 2.400–2.4835 GHz, and 0 of 2 solves of the element as it ships land inside it. The one figure that beats Apple, +0.521 dBi, is a Ø30 × 1.0 mm study puck — a different antenna on a different board, quoted once and withdrawn.
  • The board is not finished routing. 28 unconnected item(s) and 3 DRC violation(s) on the ROUTED board (KiCad 10.0.6, 2026-09-05T15:03:23); the unrouted source board it derives from still reports 83
  • Three artifacts describe three different boards. The source .kicad_pcb, the routed one derived from it, and board.py which generates them, were last written at three different times — and the gerber pack is older than all three. Every picture on this site names which of them it came from, but that does not make them agree.
  • Nothing has been sent to a factory. REFUSED, and nothing was sent. 3 of 7 gates FAIL: G3-fresh — 3 of 3 artifact(s) are older than halo_rev_a.kicad_pcb, the worst by 6,461 s (1.8 h). They were exported from ; G5-routed — 83 unconnected item(s). `fab dfm` is right not to FAIL on this — there is no copper to measure against a capab; G6-dfm — 2 rule(s) FAIL: smd_pad_min, smt_min_package The transport itself was proven — a POST with deliberately invalid credentials reached JLCPCB's gateway and came back 401 — but API access needs an approved application, and this account has no order history to be approved on.
  • C-1 — About 16 µA of the power budget is unexplained CONCERNS.md
  • C-2 — The antenna match clears by 0.39 dB CONCERNS.md
  • C-3 — The mandated sound level cannot be tested on a production line CONCERNS.md
  • C-5 — I quoted a beat-Apple antenna number the board's own source withdraws CONCERNS.md
