# Radio certification and battery safety — verbatim excerpts

Compiled by lane F, fetched 2026-09-03.

## 47 CFR § 15.212 — Modular transmitters
https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/15.212

Conditions for single-modular approval, 15.212(a)(1):
> (i) "The radio elements of the modular transmitter must have their own shielding."
> (ii) "The modular transmitter must have buffered modulation/data inputs (if such inputs are provided)"
> (iii) "The modular transmitter must have its own power supply regulation."
> (iv) "The antenna must either be permanently attached or employ a 'unique' antenna coupler"
> (v) "The modular transmitter must be tested in a stand-alone configuration"
> (vi) "The modular transmitter must be equipped with either a permanently affixed label or must be
>      capable of electronically displaying its FCC identification number."
> (vii) "The modular transmitter must comply with any specific rules or operating requirements that
>       ordinarily apply to a complete transmitter"
> (viii) RF exposure compliance with the specified FCC sections.

Host labelling when the module's own label is not visible:
> "Contains Transmitter Module FCC ID: XYZMODEL1" or "Contains FCC ID: XYZMODEL1" or any similar wording
> that expresses the same meaning.

## 47 CFR § 15.247 — 2400–2483.5 MHz digitally modulated systems
https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/15.247

> (a)(2) "The minimum 6 dB bandwidth shall be at least 500 kHz."
> (b)(3) "For systems using digital modulation in the 902-928 MHz, 2400-2483.5 MHz, and 5725-5850 MHz
>        bands: 1 Watt."
> (b)(4) "If transmitting antennas of directional gain greater than 6 dBi are used, the conducted output
>        power ... shall be reduced ... by the amount in dB that the directional gain ... exceeds 6 dBi."
> (d)    "In any 100 kHz bandwidth outside the frequency band ... the radio frequency power ... shall be at
>        least 20 dB below that in the 100 kHz bandwidth within the band."
> (e)    "Not be greater than 8 dBm in any 3 kHz band during any time interval of continuous transmission."

## FCC OET — UWB certification issues and guidance (TCB workshop)
https://transition.fcc.gov/oet/ea/presentations/files/nov17/52-UWB-Guidance-SJ.pdf

> "Whenever possible (i.e., if the UWB fundamental emission can be fully contained within the
> 5925−7250 MHz frequency band), Section 15.250 should be considered as an alternative to either Section
> 15.517 or Section 15.519."
> "Modular approval will only be considered for UWB applications under Section 15.519 requirements"

> failure to receive an acknowledgement from an associated receiver.

## CPSC — Button Cell and Coin Battery business guidance (Reese's Law, 16 CFR part 1263)
https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Business-Guidance/Button-Cell-and-Coin-Battery

Scope: "consumer products that contain button cell or coin batteries", excluding zinc-air chemistries and
toys complying with 16 CFR part 1250.

> "Battery compartments containing replaceable button cell or coin batteries must be secured such that
> they require the use of a tool or at least two independent and simultaneous hand movements to open."

Warnings required on "the packaging for the overall product", "the product itself, if practicable", and in
"accompanying instructions and manuals".

Dates: general compliance for products manufactured or imported after 19 March 2024; packaging labelling
requirements for products imported/manufactured on or after 21 September 2024. A General Certificate of
Conformity (GCC), or a Children's Product Certificate for children's products, is required.

The rule incorporates ANSI/UL 4200A-2023 by reference (Federal Register, 21 Sep 2023,
https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/09/21/2023-20334/safety-standard-for-button-cell-or-coin-batteries-and-consumer-products-containing-such-batteries).

## UL 4200A scope (UL Solutions / CSA Group summaries)
https://www.ul.com/resources/reeses-lawul-4200a-standard-safety-products-incorporating-button-batteries-or-coin-cell
https://www.csagroup.org/testing-certification/product-areas/information-communication-technology-ict/ul-4200a-button-cell-coin-batteries/

> "covers household type products that incorporate or may use button batteries or coin cell batteries"
> Battery compartments "must be secured in a way that requires the use of a tool or at least two
> independent hand movements to open"
Warning text and font heights are scaled to the principal display panel of the packaging.

## Lithium coin cell shipping
https://mydhl.express.dhl/content/dam/downloads/global/en/lithium-batteries/lithium_metal_batteries_regulations.pdf.coredownload.pdf
https://wrap-tds.com/un-3091-lithium-metal-button-cells-contained-in-equipment-by-international-air-within-limits-below/

UN 3090 = lithium metal batteries shipped alone. UN 3091 = contained in, or packed with, equipment
(PI 969 packed with; PI 970 contained in).

> "For packages containing only button cell batteries installed in equipment (including circuit boards) it
> is not required to have the statement on the waybill and the Lithium Battery Mark on the package."
