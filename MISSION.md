## The endpoint, set by Leif 2026-09-05

Verbatim: *"dont stop until its fully manufacturable and youve tested it out
wiht the jlcpcb api and sent it and gotten feedback back or a real quota"*

So "done" is no longer a self-assessment. It is **an external party looking at
our files and answering** — a real quotation, or a rejection naming what is
wrong with them. That is a far better test than any check we write, because a
factory's own preflight is the one instrument in this project that has no
incentive to agree with us.

Two things this makes explicit. A **quotation is not an order**: the endpoint is
a price and a manufacturability verdict coming back, and nothing is purchased
without Leif saying so. And the pack cannot be submitted until the board is
actually finished — the current gerbers describe a board that no longer exists,
the source board is unrouted at 83 unconnected, and the antenna is 16 % below
band. Submitting early would buy a rejection we can already predict.

## Definition of done — the factory release pack

The project is finished when `out/release/` contains all of this, every item
generated by a tool on this machine and graded PASS, never asserted:

1. **Gerbers + drill + netlist** for the Ø ~32 mm 4-layer board, DRC-clean
   against JLCPCB's published 4-layer capabilities.
2. **Schematic** as `.kicad_sch` and PDF, ERC-clean, readable by a human
   reviewer, with a hierarchical design block others can reuse.
3. **BOM** with, for every line: function, MPN, manufacturer, package, LCSC id,
   stock and price at 10 / 100 / 1 000 / 10 000, datasheet URL, and the
   alternate part if the first is out of stock.
4. **CPL / pick-and-place** in JLCPCB format with rotations verified.
5. **Panel** drawing and file, plus stackup, impedance targets and materials.
6. **Simulation evidence**: CR2032 pulse-load droop at end of life, battery-life
   estimate, sounder drive current, antenna S11 / efficiency, NFC coil tuning,
   per-layer current and voltage drop — each a `verdict.json`.
7. **Firmware**: source, build instructions, hex, the key-provisioning step, and
   a decoded Find My advertisement proving the payload is right.
8. **Mechanical**: enclosure STEP/STL, drawings, the child-safe battery door,
   assembly instructions with photos or renders, IP rating method.
9. **Test plan**: what the factory measures on every unit (current draw,
   advertisement seen, sounder dB, NFC read) with limits and fixtures.
10. **Compliance dossier**: FCC/CE route (module vs full test), DULT
    anti-stalking behaviour, Reese's Law battery-door compliance, labelling.
11. **Cost roll-up** against Apple's $29 retail, at each quantity.

Nothing ships as "should work". Every number carries the command that produced
it and the date. A CANNOT DETERMINE is a work item for an agent, never a
question for Leif.
